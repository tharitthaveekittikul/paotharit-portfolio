## My Role

Sole frontend developer — designed and built the entire marketing site from scratch. The site is the primary acquisition surface for POMPKINS's restaurant management suite, covering five product areas across 20+ pages with bilingual (EN/TH) support.

## What I Built

- Landing page with Framer Motion hero animations and AOS scroll-triggered section reveals
- Product showcase pages for POS, in-store ordering, delivery, and CRM system
- Rubtung section — a dedicated sub-section within the POMPKINS product line
- Pricing and package selection pages with interactive plan comparison
- Contact page with Google Maps integration for merchant location display
- Legal and policy pages (cookies, terms, privacy)

## Technical Highlights

- **Multi-layout routing** — three independent layout trees (marketing, Rubtung section, no-layout) sharing one Next.js codebase via route groups
- **Animation system** — Framer Motion for component-level transitions paired with AOS for scroll-triggered reveals, with no overlap in responsibility
- **Embla Carousel** — product screenshot carousels with wheel gesture support and autoplay
- **next-intl** — bilingual static content (EN/TH) with locale-aware routing throughout
